Popkey — Smart Locker Platform

Branding, web design & build for an IoT-powered smart-locker product, deployed across premium malls in Chennai & Hyderabad.

🔐
RoleUI/UX Designer & Web Developer
PlatformWix Studio
ToolsFigma · Wix Studio
DeploymentMalls · Chennai & Hyderabad

Overview

Popkey is an IoT-powered smart-locker platform offering secure, contactless access and digital key management for malls, residential and commercial spaces. I led the product's digital brand and web presence — from identity and UI design to building the live marketing site and a reusable design system aligned with the "Digital Keyvolution" positioning.

The Challenge

Launch a brand-new IoT hardware product with no existing digital identity — and make a physical smart-locker feel trustworthy, modern and effortless online, for two very different audiences: walk-in mall users scanning a QR code, and B2B partners evaluating the platform.

  • A cohesive brand identity applied across web & print
  • A responsive, mobile-first locker experience
  • A design system of reusable components & colour tokens
  • Marketing collateral for on-site mall campaigns
