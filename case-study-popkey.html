Case Study · Digital Keyvolution

Popkey — Smart Locker Platform

Branding, web design & build for an IoT-powered smart-locker product, deployed across premium malls in Chennai & Hyderabad.

RoleUI/UX Designer
PlatformWix Studio
ToolsFigma · Wix Studio
DeploymentLive · popkey.org

Overview

Popkey is an IoT-powered smart-locker platform offering secure, contactless access and digital key management for malls, residential and commercial spaces. I led the product's digital brand and web presence — from identity and UI design to building the live marketing site and a reusable design system aligned with the "Digital Keyvolution" positioning.

The Challenge

Design a complete digital experience for a new IoT smart-locker platform from the ground up. The goal was to establish a trustworthy brand, clearly communicate the product's value, and create a seamless experience for two distinct audiences: everyday shoppers using QR-based lockers and business partners evaluating the platform for deployment.

My Process

01

Discovery & Research

Competitor analysis and user research to understand customer needs, business goals, and market opportunities.

02

Product Strategy

Defined the product positioning, messaging, and information architecture to communicate the value proposition clearly to both consumers and enterprise clients.

03

Brand Identity

Designed a scalable visual identity — logo, typography, colour palette, and brand guidelines — for a consistent, recognizable presence.

04

UX & UI Design

Created user flows and high-fidelity interfaces focused on simplicity, accessibility, and a mobile-first experience.

05

Design System

Built a reusable design system with standardized components, colours, spacing, and typography to ensure consistency and support future development.

06

Development & Launch

Designed & published the live site on Wix Studio.

The Solution

  • Designed a modern, responsive marketing website that clearly communicates the product and its benefits.
  • Delivered an intuitive mobile-first experience for QR-based smart-locker interactions.
  • Established a reusable design system to improve design consistency and accelerate future development.
  • Produced supporting marketing assets for mall campaigns, product promotions, and business presentations.

Impact

Successful Product Launch

Delivered a complete digital presence for a new IoT product, enabling a successful market introduction.

Consistent Brand Experience

Created a unified visual identity that can scale across future products and marketing channels.

Improved Design Efficiency

The reusable design system reduced design effort and ensured consistency across new features and campaigns.

Real-World Deployment

The website, branding, and marketing assets were deployed to support the product's launch in shopping malls — a consistent experience for both customers and business clients.
