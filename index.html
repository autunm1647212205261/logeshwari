About Me

Designer. Developer.
Problem Solver.

I'm Logeshwari Dhanasekar, a creative and detail-oriented UI/UX Designer currently working at POPKEY Private Limited. With a strong foundation from my BCA degree and hands-on industry experience, I specialise in designing intuitive, user-friendly digital experiences.

My passion lies in transforming complex problems into elegant, accessible interfaces. I use Figma, Wix Studio, and the Adobe Creative Suite to bring ideas to life — and I'm fluent enough in HTML, CSS & JavaScript to collaborate seamlessly with developers.

Beyond screens, I've contributed to real-world branding: my graphic design work for Popkey's Smart Locker campaign has been deployed across malls in Chennai and Hyderabad.
